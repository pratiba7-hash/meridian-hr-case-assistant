# meridian-hr-case-assistant
An n8n HR case-management POC for Meridian Logistics with policy-grounded AI drafts, sensitive-case escalation, human review in Airtable, approved email responses, employee feedback and simulated Workday integration.

## Workflows
- Employee intake, privacy checks, routing and HR case logging
- Policy PDF ingestion and retrieval
- Simulated Workday employee validation
- HR-approved employee email
- Case-linked employee feedback

## Setup
Import the workflow JSON files into n8n.
Configure your own credentials, Airtable tables and policy store.
Replace configuration placeholders before running.

## Limitations
Workday uses a mock API.
The policy handbook is fictional demonstration material.
HR reviews and approves employee-facing responses.
Credentials and employee records are not included.
